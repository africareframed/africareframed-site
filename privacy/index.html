Privacy

What we collect when you request access or write to us, and why.

What we collect

Your organisation, your name and role, your email address, how you expect to use the images, and the answers you give in the partner forms.

Why

To approve access and keep in touch, and to report how the images are used, without personal details, to the funders who make new collections possible, with your consent.

How long, and your rights

We keep your details while your organisation has access and for one year after. You can ask to see, change or delete them at any time.

On your device

If you come to this site from the archive, your browser remembers it for 30 days, so the menu shows the archive’s links. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

Who to write to

hello@africareframed.com.
