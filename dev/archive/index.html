The archive on Home

Five versions of the archive in small. Version 5 is on Home since 14 November 2026; the others are kept as examples.

Choose a theme: one photograph from it appears with its record. On a phone the five names are a row to slide. Three themes, chosen to differ. Versions 1 and 2 differ in one thing only: how the keywords are set. Version 3 is another idea: a photograph over a field of words. Click the photograph, or point at a word. Version 4 sets the photographs in a band, with a slice of the one before and of the one after. Version 5 is two columns: the photograph, and a square of keywords to click.

Version 1 · the keywords as plain words

The archive

The archive holds the stories in pictures: photographs of modern African life, ready for partners to use in their own work.

Every photograph is made by a photographer on location, with the consent of the people in it. Our images are never AI-generated. The themes follow one story: people learn, put their skills to work, live connected lives in modern cities, and make culture. Three of the themes stand below, each with one photograph and the keywords it carries in the archive.
