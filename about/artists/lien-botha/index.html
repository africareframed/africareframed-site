Shown at Øksnehallen

Lien Botha

Photographs of place and memory, from a practice that spans photography, printmaking and sculpture.

Lien Botha was born in Gauteng, South Africa, in 1961. She worked as a press photographer for the daily Beeld before she moved to Cape Town in 1984, where she took a degree in fine art at the Michaelis School of Fine Art in 1988.

She works in photography, printmaking, painting and sculpture, and returns to the places of daily life to find in their details the traces of personal and shared memory.

Parrot Jungle

Made in 2009: a disrupted road trip on the coast road between Betty’s Bay and Cape Town, returning to the places missed on the way to somewhere else: roadkill, people walking, stray dogs, the road gone under the rain. Botha calls it a drive-by revisited. Part of the series was shown at the Bamako Encounters in 2011.
