The archive on Home

A simpler archive in small, on trial. Home itself is unchanged.

Choose a theme: one photograph from it appears with its record. On a phone the five names are a row to slide. The two versions differ in one thing only: how the keywords are set.

Version 1 · the keywords as plain words

The archive

The archive holds the stories in pictures: photographs of modern African life, ready for partners to use in their own work.

Every photograph is made by a photographer on location, with the consent of the people in it. Our images are never AI-generated. The themes follow one story: people learn, put their skills to work, live connected lives in modern cities, and make culture. One photograph from each theme stands below, with the keywords it carries in the archive.
