Request access

The archive is free for selected non-profit and public partners: NGOs, media, educational institutions, embassies, ministries and development finance institutions. Companies get access through a collection sponsorship: a sponsor gets the whole archive, under the same partner licence as everyone else.

Read the rules for using the images first. Until this form is connected, requests go through the archive’s request form.

What happens next

  1. We read your request and reply within a working day.
  2. You get a welcome email with your login; the account opens for the address you give.
  3. You log in collect in the Lightbox and download for each use, free.

Companies get access through a collection sponsorship; write to partnerships@africareframed.com.

Thank you

Your request is with us. We reply within a working day, and the welcome email that follows opens your account.
