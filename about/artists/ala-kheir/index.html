Ala Kheir

Photographs of cities, landscapes and everyday life, in Sudan and beyond.

Ala Kheir was born in Nyala, in south-western Sudan, in 1985, and has photographed since he bought his first camera in 2005.

He keeps to no single subject and photographs everything around him, inspired by the American photographer Robert Adams. His work has been shown at the Addis Foto Fest, with a solo exhibition in 2012, and in New York, Kuala Lumpur and Khartoum.
