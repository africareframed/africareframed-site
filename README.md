# africareframed.com

The public site of Africa Reframed. The archive lives on PhotoDeck at archive.africareframed.com, behind a partner login; this site presents the initiative and leads partners to it.

**Live wireframe, version 4 (6 November 2026).** The five key pages (Home, the theme pages, the collection page, Partners, Partner portal) are arranged by the page priorities in "Website – Site map and user flows", section 6. Grey boxes with a cross are images still to choose, at the size and proportion they will have. Grey bars are text still to write, and yellow notes say what goes there. Real words appear only where they already exist in the master documents. "Clean view" in the yellow strip hides the notes, so a page can be shown as a layout.

- **Build:** `python3 src/build.py` writes all 39 pages, `404.html`, `assets/site.css` and `assets/share-default.png` into the repository root. GitHub Actions publishes to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
- **Structure:** follows "Website – Site map and user flows" (05 Website on the Drive), 16 pages from 9 templates. Home; Themes plus five theme pages; Collections plus the House Collection; Partners; Partner portal; Request access; About; Press room; Contact; Privacy.
- **The grid** (step 1 of the website build, 8 November 2026; starting values, locked after step 2): desktop 12 columns, 32 px gutters, 64 px margins, content up to 1,312 px (80 px columns at 1,440 px, the guide's hero format); tablet (768–1,023 px) 8 columns, 24 px gutters, 32 px margins; phone 4 columns, 16 px gutters and margins. The header is 128 px tall with the logo at 192 px (two columns and a gutter) on desktop and tablet, 64 px with the logo at 128 px on phones. Running text caps at 36 em. Photographs relate to the grid in three ways: full bleed (`.bleed`), on the columns (`.cols` with `.c1-12`, `.c6-12`, …), or from a column line off one edge (`.c5-off`, the Opening: from column 5 on desktop, from column 2 below, where the words move under the photograph). The values live in `GRID` and `GRID_CSS_VARS` in `src/build.py` and reach the stylesheet as custom properties; the **Grid** button in the wireframe strip, the key G or `?grid` in the address draws the columns and margins with a readout. Listed on `/dev/` and proposed as section U of "Design guide – review and changes".
- **Header:** defined once, in `src/build.py`. It reads Themes · Collections · Partners · About | Log in, where Log in opens the Partner portal (About moved up from the footer on 9 November 2026; it stays in the footer too). The archive keeps the same header and adds its own tools (Browse images, Lightbox). Keep the two headers pixel-identical.
- **Footer:** the about line, then About · Press · Contact · Privacy · Request access · Terms (the archive's terms).
- **Links into the archive** carry `src=main-…` so their origin is visible in the archive's statistics. Theme pages use interim searches until each theme has its own keyword (Keywording Guide, section 8b).
- **Link previews:** every page has Open Graph tags. The share image is the logo on black until real images are chosen.
- **Development page:** every build also writes `/dev/`: the order of work, every page with its template, state and what is still to fill, the masters on the Drive and claude.ai, every link into the archive, the launch checklist and recent changes. It is a view, not a log: decisions and the log stay on the Drive. Set a page's state in `STATE` in `src/build.py`.
- **Quality rules** (02 Brand on the Drive; applied 11 November 2026): the site is held to them by the build and by a gate.
  - *Type:* six sizes and three weights, as named styles in the stylesheet (`--fs-display` 64, `--fs-title` 44, 32 below desktop, `--fs-head` 22, `--fs-text` 17, `--fs-small` 13; weights 400, 700, 300). Nothing else sets a size. `/dev/type/` shows the scale.
  - *Text length:* every module checks its words against `LIMITS` in `src/build.py` (from "Website – modules"); a text over its limit is listed on `/dev/` and fails the gate. The page budgets are in `src/check.py`.
  - *Images:* every module shows an image through `frame()`, which puts its credit line beneath it. No image without its credit.
  - *Only what exists:* a module whose data does not exist yet (the in-use numbers, partners by name, uses, news, a theme's sponsor) is not built. Enter the data in `PARTNERS_NAMED`, `USES`, `NEWS`, `THEME_SPONSORS` and `MOMENTUM`, and the module appears. `/dev/modules/` still shows every module with placeholders.
  - *The yellow* (`--accent`, the guide's #eacb84) has one job: the way into the archive (`.button.archive`, `.panel.archive`).
  - *The gate:* `python3 src/build.py && python3 src/check.py` opens every page at 1,440, 834 and 390 px and measures it against the rules that can be counted. Run it before every push; nothing is published that makes a number worse. It needs Playwright with Chromium.
- **Fonts:** DM Sans is served from `assets/fonts` (SIL Open Font License; the licence is beside the files). No request goes to another company's server.
- **Launch switch:** `LAUNCH = True` in `src/build.py` removes `/dev/`, the wireframe strip and `noindex`. It does not decide which sections show: that follows the data (above).
- **Deploy:** the workflow runs `python3 src/build.py` before publishing, so the live site always matches `src/`.
- **Not indexed:** `noindex` stays on until launch on the real domain.
- **Brief:** "Website – Brief and build plan" (Google Doc, 05 Website on the Africa Reframed Drive).
