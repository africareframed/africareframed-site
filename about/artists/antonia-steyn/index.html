Antonia Steyn

Portraits of South African figures, made with a strong sense of staging.

Antonia Steyn graduated from the University of Cape Town in 2000 and has worked as a professional photographer since 2005, in portraiture and in campaigns for advertising clients.

Her portrait of the satirist Pieter-Dirk Uys won her Media24 Photographer of the Year in 2007, and her triptych Steyn & De Villiers the Vuleka Art Award in 2011.
