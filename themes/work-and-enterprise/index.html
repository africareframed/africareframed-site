What it shows

Tradespeople, cooks, farmers, shopkeepers and young professionals at work, with the tools and the places of their trade: a bicycle repair, a carpenter’s workshop, a market stall, a café meeting, tea fields. For a partner working in skills, enterprise or agriculture the theme shows value being created by people who are good at what they do.
