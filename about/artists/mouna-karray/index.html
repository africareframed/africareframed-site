Mouna Karray

Photographs on identity, memory and constraint.

Mouna Karray was born in Sfax, Tunisia, in 1970, and took an MA in image media at the Tokyo Institute of Polytechnics and Arts in 2002. She lives and works between Sfax and Paris.

Her work has been shown at the Bamako Encounters in 2011 and at Photoquai, at the Musée du Quai Branly in Paris, in 2009.

Text and series lines are draft, from the profile on commerceandculture.org. Website to find.
