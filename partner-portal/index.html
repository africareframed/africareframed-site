How it works

Find → collect → license → use

Images are always free for partners. Each use has its own licence: it comes with the download, and it tells us where the image will appear.

Find

Search by keyword, theme and place

Open Browse images and use the search tab. Every image carries keywords for what it shows, its theme, its city and its country. A click on a keyword under an image shows every image that shares it.

The five themes are listed on the House Collection page, each with a link into the archive.

Collect and license

Gather a selection

Under an image, press Add to selection. Your selection is under Lightbox in the menu, and the selections you save are listed in My account.

Download an image

Press Download (free licence) and choose how you will use the image: in a report or publication, a funding application, a presentation, on your website or social media, in the press, at an exhibition or event, in teaching, or another use that you describe.

In the comment, name the project or the publication. The original file, 6,000 pixels wide, comes to your email together with the terms.

Why a licence, when it is free?

The images are rights-managed: each use is agreed, and nobody holds a stock of images for uses not yet known. So every use has its own licence, free of charge, and a new use means a new download.

The licence tells us where the image appears. That is how we show the people and organisations who fund the archive that it is used.

Use

What you may do, at a glance
  • Use the image for the purpose you chose at download, anywhere in the world.
  • Crop it and adjust its colour. Change nothing else in it.
  • Credit every use.
  • Do not resell the image or pass the licence on.
  • Do not use it in political campaigning or to advertise products.
  • Do not place the people shown in a degrading or misleading context.
  • Do not use it to train AI models.

Before you publish
  • The credit line stands on or beside the image.
  • The caption says what the image shows, and where.
  • The use is the one you chose at download. For a new use, download the image again.
